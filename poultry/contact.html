Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our farm and services.

Do you offer farm tours?

Yes! We offer guided farm tours every Saturday from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Please contact us at least one week in advance to schedule your visit.

What are your wholesale prices?

Wholesale pricing depends on order volume and delivery location. Contact our sales team at wholesale@kvpoultry.com for a custom quote.

Do you ship eggs across the country?

Currently, we deliver locally within a 50-mile radius. For distant orders, we recommend contacting us to discuss special arrangements.

Are your products organic certified?

Our birds are raised on organic feed and in humane conditions. We're working towards official organic certification and should have it completed by Q3 2026.

Can I visit the farm without a scheduled tour?

For safety and biosecurity reasons, we require advance notice for all farm visits. Please call or email us to arrange a time.
