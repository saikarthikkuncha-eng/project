Our Growth Journey

From a humble family venture to a regional market leader in clean agriculture production.

2016

Farm founded with a modest single-coop setup housing 500 heritage laying hens. Operational focus centered purely on local direct-to-consumer farmgate sales.

2019

Constructed our first fully integrated industrial tier feeding line and computerized grading workshop. Scaled flock size sustainably up to 15,000 active layers.

2023

Acquired state environmental safety approvals and certified clean-feed processing plant designation. Initiated institutional commercial B2B supply logistics pipelines.

2026

Pioneering smart farm zero-carbon infrastructure housing over 100,000 birds, utilizing specialized telemetry sensors and automated bio-defense protocols.
